Starting My Web Development Journey

October 3, 2026 by Jonathan Payne

All my life I have been the creative type. I enjoy making things and learning new skills and using knowledge to help others. Whether it be using my life experiences to help others with what they are going through, or using my skills to do something to lighten the mood. As a musician, using my skills of creating and performing music for others enjoyment is something I have always enjoyed and am passionate about. Now, as an upcomming programmer, I am learning to use my skills to create websites, applications, and games that can help others in their daily lives.

Learning about web development has been a challenging but rewarding experience. I have learned about different programming languages, frameworks, and tools that are used in the industry. I have also learned about the importance of user experience and accessibility in web design. I am excited to continue learning and improving my skills as a web developer, and I hope to use my knowledge to create meaningful and impactful projects in the future.

Finding Inspiration in Everyday Life

September 28, 2026 by Jonathan Payne

Inspiration can come from many different places, many unexpected. Sometimes taking a break from our daily routines and spending some time outside helps me to think more clearly. I often notice colours, shapes and different designs around me that give me ideas for projects. Sometimes I find myself getting lost in the most mundane of things, but even the differences in how some leaves o a tree curl can spark inspiration, its just to seize the moment.

I have learned that creativity does not always happen when I am trying to spark it on my own. Taking time to relax can help me return to my work with a fresh perspective and help me have a clear mind to solve my problems effectively. Through all my expereinces, I have learned that sometimes the greatest thing that helps is a change of pace and scenery.
